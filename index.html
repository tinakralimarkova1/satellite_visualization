Satellite Data Visualizations

Satellites launched worldwide from 1957 through February 22, 2026.

Source & methodology

Source: Jonathan McDowell satellite catalog

Data updated: February 22, 2026.

What is counted: Each cataloged satellite or payload is counted as one satellite launched.

Country assignment: Satellites are assigned to the country of the first listed owner. When owner information is unavailable, the catalog’s state field is used.

USSR/Russia and multinational ownership: Soviet Union records are combined with Russia. For missions with multiple owners, the satellite is assigned only to the first listed owner.

Purpose categories:

Observation: Imaging, radar imaging, meteorology, Earth and space science, astronomy, geodesy, and calibration.

Communication: Communications satellites.

Geolocation: Navigation satellites.

Armamentation: Weapons and targets.

All Other Categories: All remaining or uncategorized purposes.
